==== *Synopsis*
[.removed]#This project aims to develop a centralized online platform that simplifies the scheduling of medical appointments for patients, doctors and clinics in Puerto Rico. By replacing inefficient phone-based systems with real-time availability, organized search features, and user-friendly booking tools, the platform seeks to reduce waiting times and administrative workload while improving access to timely healthcare. Ultimately, the system provides a scalable and coordinated solution to address the growing demand for medical services and modernize the appointment scheduling process.#

[.added]#This project addresses the difficulty of coordinating medical appointments
within a constrained healthcare environment in Puerto Rico, where access to
care is limited by provider availability, fragmented scheduling practices, and
administrative overhead. The objective is to model and prototype a structured
approach to appointment coordination that reflects the constraints and
interactions observed in the domain.#

[.added]#To achieve this, the project requires multiple types of work spanning analysis,
design, implementation, and validation. Domain engineering activities are used
to identify and formalize key concepts such as patients, providers, appointments,
and scheduling constraints, ensuring that the system reflects real-world
practices. Requirements engineering translates these domain observations into
precise, traceable requirements that define system behavior.#

[.added]#System design and architecture planning establish how scheduling logic,
interactions, and data structures are organized, followed by implementation
activities that develop prototype-level frontend and backend components.
Supporting this, database design is used to represent domain entities and their
relationships in a consistent and extensible manner.#

[.added]#Verification and validation are performed through multiple approaches. These
include Test-Driven Development and Behavior-Driven Development to
guide implementation, as well as load testing, mutation testing, and formal
verification techniques such as Hoare logic to evaluate correctness and
robustness. Additional validation activities include consistency checks,
traceability verification, and walkthrough-based analysis of domain scenarios.#

[.added]#Throughout the project, continuous documentation, version control, and team
coordination activities support development by maintaining clarity, alignment,
and traceability across all components. These activities are carried out in
parallel and iteratively, ensuring that the system evolves in a structured and
verifiable manner toward a coherent representation of the scheduling domain.#
