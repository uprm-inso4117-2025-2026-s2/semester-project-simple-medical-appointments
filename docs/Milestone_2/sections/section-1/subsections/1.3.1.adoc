===== [.added]#Scope#

[.added]#The scope of this project lies within the broader domain of healthcare
service coordination, specifically focusing on the organization and
management of outpatient medical appointments. This includes the
interaction between patients, providers, and administrative entities in
the process of requesting, scheduling, modifying, and attending medical
consultations.#

[.added]#Within this domain, scheduling is influenced by constraints such as
provider availability, appointment capacity, referral requirements, and
patient demand. These interactions occur within a larger healthcare
ecosystem characterized by fragmented communication channels, varying
levels of digital adoption, and resource limitations. The project is
situated in the context of Puerto Rico, where healthcare access is
affected by workforce availability, geographic distribution of services,
and administrative coordination practices.#

[.added]#The scope therefore encompasses the general problem space of how medical
appointments are coordinated, managed, and accessed within a constrained
healthcare environment.#


===== [.added]#Span#

[.added]#The span of this project is limited to the design and prototyping of a
system that models appointment scheduling within outpatient, private
clinic settings. The focus is on representing the core interactions,
constraints, and entities involved in scheduling consultations between
patients and providers.#

[.added]#The project is restricted to an academic prototype and does not extend to
full-scale system deployment or real-world integration. It does not
include hospital systems, emergency care coordination, or large-scale
healthcare infrastructure. Additionally, the system will not interact
with external services such as insurance providers, electronic health
record systems, or third-party scheduling platforms. No real patient data 
or protected health information will be used. 
All data represented within the system will be simulated for the purpose of
demonstrating scheduling behavior and system interactions.#

[.added]#The span is therefore confined to a controlled, simplified representation
of outpatient appointment scheduling, focusing on core domain behavior
rather than complete system implementation.#
