==== *Narrative*

[.changed]#A patient identifies a needed specialty and contacts a clinic to request an appointment. Each provider keeps a schedule of discrete time slots. Clinic staff match the request to an open slot and create an appointment that links one patient, one provider, a consultation type, and a time.#

The process begins when a patient needs medical attention and identifies the appropriate specialty. The patient contacts a clinic or doctor to request an appointment. Each doctor maintains a schedule that defines available time slots for consultations. Clinic staff review the schedule and assign an available time slot, creating an appointment between one patient and one doctor at a specific date and time.

As appointments fill the schedule, fewer time slots remain available. In high demand specialties, patients may experience significant waiting time before receiving care. If a patient cancels an appointment, the time slot becomes available again and may be reassigned to another patient.

[.added]#Because providers work across locations and schedules shift, clinics must continuously reconcile calendars, redistribute freed slots, and keep patients informed. The domain hangs together through the lifecycle of a time slot—published, requested, confirmed (sometimes conditionally), potentially reassigned, and finally completed or marked absent—under operational-hour constraints and policies that prevent double booking.#
