==== *Narrative*

[.changed]#The medical appointment scheduling domain involves the coordination of patients, caregivers, healthcare providers, doctors, clinics, schedules, time slots, and appointments to organize consultation services. A healthcare provider, such as a doctor, offers consultations within a clinic and defines availability through a schedule composed of discrete time slots constrained by operational hours. Each time slot has an availability state and may transition through reserved, canceled, or expired conditions as part of its lifecycle.#

[.changed]#The process begins when a patient, or a caregiver acting on their behalf, requests an appointment for a specific consultation type. Clinic staff review the schedules of available providers and reserve an appropriate time slot, creating an appointment that links the patient, provider, clinic, and scheduled time. The appointment is assigned a booking status, such as pending or confirmed, depending on whether constraints like insurance authorization or referral requirements apply. A patient may hold zero or more appointments but cannot hold multiple active appointments with the same provider simultaneously.#

[.changed]#As appointments are confirmed, time slots transition from available to reserved, reducing overall availability within the schedule. A patient or clinic may cancel an appointment, triggering a cancellation event that returns the associated time slot to an available state. Patients may also reschedule appointments by releasing one reserved time slot and reserving another. In cases where no immediate availability exists, a patient may join a waitlist, which maintains an ordered collection of patients awaiting assignment. When a time slot becomes available, an assign waitlisted patient event may allocate the slot to the next eligible patient according to defined ordering constraints.#

[.changed]#Clinic staff manage schedules manually, coordinating multiple providers across different consultation types while ensuring domain constraints such as no double booking, consultation duration integrity, and operational hour restrictions are maintained. If a provider becomes unavailable, scheduled appointments must be rescheduled to alternative time slots or providers. Additionally, appointments may result in no-show events when patients fail to attend without prior cancellation, leaving reserved time slots unused. These interactions define how booking status, availability, and scheduling constraints evolve over time within the domain.#
