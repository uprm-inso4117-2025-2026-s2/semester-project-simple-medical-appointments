==== *Domain Requirements*

[.changed]
===== Appointment Slot Requirements

[discrete]
====== Slot Capacity

*Domain basis:*  
Consultation periods are limited by time, staff, and facility availability.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-SLOT-F-01]]REQ-SLOT-F-01: The system must associate each appointment slot with a finite capacity defined by the clinic or provider.

* [[REQ-SLOT-F-02]]REQ-SLOT-F-02: The system must prevent allocation of appointments beyond the defined capacity of a slot.

[discrete]
====== Appointment Association

*Domain basis:*  
Consultations occur between a patient and a healthcare professional and form part of the patient’s medical history.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-APPT-F-01]]REQ-APPT-F-01: The system must associate each scheduled appointment with exactly one patient and at least one provider.

* [[REQ-APPT-F-02]]REQ-APPT-F-02: The system must maintain the association between a patient and their scheduled appointments over time.

[discrete]
===== Provider Availability Requirements

[discrete]
====== Availability Representation

*Domain basis:*  
Providers work within defined schedules that vary by clinic and day.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-AVAIL-F-02]]REQ-AVAIL-F-02: The system must ensure that appointments only occur within declared availability intervals.

[discrete]
===== Appointment Confirmation Requirements

[discrete]
====== Confirmation Rules

*Domain basis:*  
Some consultations depend on insurance authorization, referrals, or administrative review, so provisional bookings do not guarantee consultation.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-CONFIRM-F-01]]REQ-CONFIRM-F-01: The system must represent whether an appointment requires confirmation before becoming final.

* [[REQ-CONFIRM-F-02]]REQ-CONFIRM-F-02: The system must prevent appointments requiring confirmation from being treated as finalized until confirmation has occurred.

[discrete]
===== Appointment Reassignment Requirements

[discrete]
====== Cancellation and Reassignment

*Domain basis:*  
Canceled time periods become available for reassignment to reduce patient waiting time.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-REASSIGN-F-01]]REQ-REASSIGN-F-01: The system must provide means to reassign released appointment slots to other patients.

[discrete]
===== Attendance Tracking Requirements

*Domain basis:*  
Clinics monitor attendance to evaluate workload, reliability, and resource use; past participation influences future scheduling and clinical planning.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-ATTEND-F-01]]REQ-ATTEND-F-01: The system must record whether a patient attended a scheduled appointment.

* [[REQ-ATTEND-F-02]]REQ-ATTEND-F-02: The system must preserve attendance records as part of appointment history.

[discrete]
===== Referral and Authorization Requirements

*Domain basis:*  
Certain specialist visits cannot occur without prior approval or referral.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-REFERRAL-F-01]]REQ-REFERRAL-F-01: The system must represent when a consultation depends on an external referral or authorization.

* [[REQ-REFERRAL-F-02]]REQ-REFERRAL-F-02: The system must prevent scheduling of consultations requiring referral until such referral is recorded.

[discrete]
===== Appointment Sequencing Requirements

*Domain basis:*  
Consultations occur in sequence and cannot overlap for the same provider.

*Requirements:*

* [[REQ-SEQUENCE-F-01]]REQ-SEQUENCE-F-01: The system must represent appointment time as an ordered temporal position within a schedule.

* [[REQ-SEQUENCE-F-02]]REQ-SEQUENCE-F-02: The system must prevent overlapping appointments for a single provider.
