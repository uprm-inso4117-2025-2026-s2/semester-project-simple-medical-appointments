==== *Events, Actions, and Behaviors*

===== Appointment slot published

- *Type:* Event  
- *Definition:* A clinic or provider makes an appointment slot available to the community.  
- *Trigger:* A provider defines availability in terms of time, location, and service.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* A new appointment slot exists with status *available* and is visible to patients for discovery and booking.

===== Appointment requested

- *Type:* Action → Event  
- *Definition:* A patient expresses intent to book a specific appointment slot.  
- *Trigger:* A patient selects a slot and submits a request.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* A request record exists; the appointment enters a confirmation or waitlist process depending on constraints.

===== Appointment confirmed

- *Type:* Event  
- *Definition:* An appointment request is validated and officially scheduled.  
- *Trigger:* Clinic approval, automatic validation, or completion of confirmation workflow.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* The slot status becomes *reserved*; the appointment is part of the clinic schedule and reminders may be scheduled.  
- Reference to sketch: Clinics maintain structured schedules once slots are assigned.  

===== Appointment canceled

- *Type:* Action → Event  
- *Definition:* A previously confirmed or requested appointment is withdrawn.  
- *Trigger:* A patient or clinic cancels the appointment.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* The slot becomes *available* again; downstream processes (e.g., waitlist handling) may be triggered.

===== Slot reassigned to waiting patient

- *Type:* Event  
- *Definition:* An available slot is allocated to a patient from a waiting list.  
- *Trigger:* A cancellation or new availability combined with existing waitlist demand.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* The slot becomes *reserved* for the selected patient; notification is issued.

===== Provider availability modified

- *Type:* Action → Event  
- *Definition:* A provider updates their working schedule or availability constraints.  
- *Trigger:* A provider changes working hours, location, or service capacity.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* Availability records are updated; conflicting appointments may require rescheduling or notification.

===== Appointment reminder issued

- *Type:* Action → Event  
- *Definition:* A reminder notification is generated for an upcoming appointment.  
- *Trigger:* The scheduled appointment time approaches based on configured timing rules.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* A notification record exists; the patient has been informed through available communication channels.  
- Reference to sketch: Staff sometimes send manual reminders from personal phones.  

===== Patient marked absent

- *Type:* Event  
- *Definition:* A patient fails to attend a scheduled appointment.  
- *Trigger:* The appointment time passes without attendance confirmation.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* The appointment is marked as *no-show*; attendance metrics are updated.

===== Referral recorded for consultation

- *Type:* Event (Entity creation)  
- *Definition:* A referral authorizing a patient to access a consultation is registered.  
- *Trigger:* An external provider issues and records a referral.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* A referral entity exists; eligibility constraints for booking are satisfied.  
- Reference to sketch: Referral requirements introduce coordination steps.  

===== Patient joins waitlist

- *Type:* Action → Event  
- *Definition:* A patient registers interest in future appointment availability when no slots are currently available.  
- *Trigger:* A patient opts into a waitlist due to lack of availability.  
- *Observable Condition / Resulting State:* A waitlist entry exists; the patient becomes eligible for reassignment when slots open.

===== Conceptual Function Signatures

*Purpose:* These conceptual functions describe the core domain operations from a system-independent perspective, including their purpose, required inputs, outputs, and dependencies. They map to the domain events and actions defined above.

====== Publish Slot

----
publishSlot(timeSlotCatalog: TimeSlotCatalog, provider: Provider, slot: TimeSlot): Result TimeSlotId Error
----

- *Description:* Adds a new appointment slot to the provider’s availability and makes it discoverable.
- *Conceptual Inputs:* Time slot catalog, provider, time slot definition.
- *Conceptual Output:* Identifier of the created slot or an error.
- *Dependencies:* Requires provider authorization and valid scheduling constraints.
- *Related Domain Events/Actions:* Corresponds to *Appointment slot published* (event).

====== Request Appointment

----
requestAppointment(appointmentBook: AppointmentBook, patient: Patient, slotId: TimeSlotId): Result AppointmentId Error
----

- *Description:* Registers a patient’s request to book a specific appointment slot.
- *Conceptual Inputs:* Appointment book, patient, slot identifier.
- *Conceptual Output:* Appointment identifier or error.
- *Dependencies:* Slot must exist and be available.
- *Related Domain Events/Actions:* Corresponds to *Appointment requested* (action/event).

====== Confirm Appointment

----
confirmAppointment(appointmentBook: AppointmentBook, timeSlotCatalog: TimeSlotCatalog, referralRegistry: ReferralRegistry, insuranceRegistry: InsuranceRegistry, appointmentId: AppointmentId): Result Appointment Error
----

- *Description:* Validates and confirms an appointment based on constraints such as referrals and insurance.
- *Conceptual Inputs:* Appointment book, slot catalog, referral and insurance registries, appointment ID.
- *Conceptual Output:* Confirmed appointment or error.
- *Dependencies:* Requires eligibility validation and slot availability.
- *Related Domain Events/Actions:* Corresponds to *Appointment confirmed* (event).

====== Cancel Appointment

----
cancelAppointment(appointmentBook: AppointmentBook, timeSlotCatalog: TimeSlotCatalog, appointmentId: AppointmentId): Result Unit Error
----

- *Description:* Cancels an existing appointment and releases its associated slot.
- *Conceptual Inputs:* Appointment book, slot catalog, appointment ID.
- *Conceptual Output:* Success or failure.
- *Dependencies:* Appointment must exist and be cancelable.
- *Related Domain Events/Actions:* Corresponds to *Appointment canceled* (action/event).

====== Reschedule Appointment

----
rescheduleAppointment(appointmentBook: AppointmentBook, timeSlotCatalog: TimeSlotCatalog, appointmentId: AppointmentId, newSlotId: TimeSlotId): Result Unit Error
----

- *Description:* Changes an existing appointment to a different available slot.
- *Conceptual Inputs:* Appointment book, slot catalog, appointment ID, new slot ID.
- *Conceptual Output:* Success or failure.
- *Dependencies:* New slot must be available and compatible.
- *Related Domain Events/Actions:* Impacts *Appointment confirmed* and *Provider availability modified*.

====== Modify Availability

----
modifyAvailability(schedule: Schedule, provider: Provider, range: DateTimeRange, policy: Policy): Result Unit Error

- *Description:* Updates a provider’s working hours or scheduling constraints.
- *Conceptual Inputs:* Schedule, provider, time range, policy.

- *Dependencies:* Must respect scheduling policies and avoid conflicts.
- *Related Domain Events/Actions:* Corresponds to *Provider availability modified* (action/event).
