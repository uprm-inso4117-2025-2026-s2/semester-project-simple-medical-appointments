I hit redial 23 times before someone picked up at 8:49. *Patient (Miguel, 34, construction worker)*:  
[.changed]#"My shift on the Aguadilla site runs 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. with a 30-minute lunch. The next day I stopped by the clinic at 6:10 p.m.; the front door was already locked. I ended up making a third trip on Saturday morning to ask in person if any cancellations had opened."#

*Patient (Natalia, university student)*:

===== Clinic Administrative Staff

*Clinic Administrative Assistant (Laura, small family clinic)*:  
[.changed]#"The phones start ringing at 8:00 a.m. and do not stop. We book while answering another call on hold. When someone cancels, we flip through a notebook to remember who asked for an earlier slot and start dialing numbers from memory. We also keep a spreadsheet because we do not trust the notebook alone."#  

*Clinic Administrator (Jose, regional clinic network)*:  
[.changed]#"We do not have visibility into the schedules of our other locations. Patients ask if the Bayamon site has something sooner, but I cannot see their calendar, so I can only tell them to call over there."#

===== Doctors / Providers

*Doctor (Dr. Rivera, cardiologist)*:  
[.changed]#"My calendar shows full for weeks, but some days I get two no-shows, and other days the stress tests run long and everything slides. Patients get frustrated, but I cannot predict which consult will take 10 minutes and which will take 30."#  

*Doctor (Dr. Morales, general practitioner)*:  
[.changed]#"Some patients book two appointments with me and another doctor the same week and then cancel the day before. I get why they do it—they are afraid of losing the slot—but it makes the week unpredictable."#
