==== *Terminology*

[NOTE]
====
Each term below is derived from raw observations in the Domain Rough Sketch (2.1.1) and refined through concept analysis. Terms are presented with their classification ([.changed]#entity, action, event, or behavior#) and the phase in which they appear (domain, requirements, implementation).

Terms are organized into Domain Nouns, Domain Verbs, and Domain Constraints to align with ubiquitous language principles.
====


[discrete]
===== [.changed]#Domain Nouns (entities)#

NOTE: [.changed]#*Actor* is not used as a separate concept classification. It is a role annotation for entities that can initiate actions.#

*Patient* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
A person seeking medical consultation or treatment. A patient may request, confirm, cancel, or reschedule appointments. [.added]#Role: actor.#
[.small]*Derived from: Carmen’s redial marathon to secure a May slot (5 Feb 2026) and Natalia’s stalled waitlist (Feb 2026).*

*Caregiver* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
A person managing appointments on behalf of a patient who cannot coordinate independently. [.added]#Role: actor.#
[.small]*Derived from: Elena coordinating three linked visits for her mother in March 2026.*

*Healthcare Provider* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
A licensed professional offering medical consultations (e.g., general practitioner, specialist). [.added]#Role: actor.#
[.small]*Derived from: Dr. Rivera’s variable consult durations and no-shows (Feb 2026 sketch).*

*Doctor* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
A specific type of healthcare provider with a medical degree. Doctors may have different scheduling constraints than other providers. [.added]#Role: actor.#
[.small]*Derived from: provider-level constraints voiced by Dr. Rivera and Dr. Morales (Feb 2026 sketches).*

*Clinic* :: (entity, domain)
A healthcare facility that hosts providers and manages appointment availability within defined operational constraints.

*Appointment* :: (entity, domain)
[.changed]#A scheduled consultation record linking a patient, provider, clinic, and a reserved time slot.# [.added]#Note: "time block" (used informally in some sketches) is not a distinct entity; it refers to the reserved Time Slot associated with an appointment.#

*Time Slot* :: (entity, domain)
A discrete, reservable interval in a provider’s schedule. A time slot may be available, reserved, canceled, or expired.

*Schedule* :: (entity, domain)
The structured allocation of time slots associated with a provider or clinic.

*Consultation Type* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
A classification of medical visit (e.g., follow-up, first-time visit, procedure) that influences duration and constraints.

*Waitlist* :: (entity, domain)
An ordered collection of patients waiting for a time slot when no immediate availability exists.
[.small]*Derived from: Natalia’s opaque position after joining a waitlist (Feb 2026).*

*Cancellation* :: (event, domain)
The release of a previously reserved appointment before its scheduled time.

*No-Show* :: (event, domain)
The absence of a patient at a scheduled appointment without prior cancellation.
[.small]*Derived from: Dr. Rivera’s no-show fluctuation (Feb 2026 sketch).*

*Insurance Authorization* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
[.changed]#A formal approval record issued by an insurer that must be obtained before certain appointments can be confirmed.#
[.small]*Derived from: Marcos waiting on insurer portals before confirming slots (Feb 2026 sketch).*

*Referral* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
A directive from one provider requiring a patient to consult another provider before treatment.

[.added]#*Referral Recorded*# :: [.added]#(event, domain)#
[.added]#The moment at which a referral from an external provider is registered in the system, making the patient eligible to book the referred consultation.#

*Availability* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
The condition of a time slot being open for reservation.

*Booking Status* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
The current condition of an appointment (pending, confirmed, canceled, completed).

*Operational Hours* :: [.changed]#(entity, domain)#
The defined time boundaries within which a clinic accepts or schedules appointments.
[.small]*Derived from: Miguel finding the clinic closed after his shift (21–22 Jan 2026).*

[discrete]
===== Domain Constraints (invariant business rules)

*No Double Booking* :: [.added]#(behavior, domain)#
A single time slot cannot be reserved by more than one patient simultaneously.
[.small]*Derived from: Dr. Morales observing duplicate bookings that cancel late (Feb 2026 sketch).*

*Single Active Appointment per Provider Constraint* :: [.added]#(behavior, domain)#
A patient may not hold multiple concurrent active appointments with the same provider.

*Operational Hour Restriction* :: [.added]#(behavior, domain)#
Appointments cannot be scheduled outside clinic operational hours.
[.small]*Derived from: Miguel’s inability to book after 6:00 p.m. when the clinic door was locked (22 Jan 2026).*

*Consultation Duration Integrity* :: [.added]#(behavior, domain)#
The allocated time slot must match the required duration for the selected consultation type.

*Waitlist Ordering Integrity* :: [.added]#(behavior, domain)#
Waitlisted patients must be assigned based on defined priority rules.

*Authorization Dependency* :: [.added]#(behavior, domain)#
Certain appointments cannot transition to confirmed status without required insurance approval.
[.small]*Derived from: Marcos losing slots while waiting for insurer authorization (Feb 2026 sketch).*

[discrete]
===== Core Domain Flow (Appointment Lifecycle)

. A provider defines availability through structured time slots within operational hours.
. A patient requests an appointment by selecting an available time slot.
. If constraints apply (authorization or referral), the appointment enters pending status.
. Upon confirmation, the slot becomes reserved.
. If canceled, the slot returns to available state.
. If a waitlist exists, the next patient may be assigned automatically.
. The appointment either completes successfully or becomes a no-show.
. Historical records preserve appointment outcomes for traceability.
