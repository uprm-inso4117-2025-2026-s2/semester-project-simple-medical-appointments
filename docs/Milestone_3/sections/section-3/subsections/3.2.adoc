=== *Validation and Verification*

This section documents structured Validation and Verification (V&V) activities to ensure:

- Domain concepts reflect real-world clinical scheduling practices.
- Requirements are consistent, precise, and traceable to domain concepts.
- Documentation quality satisfies Milestone 1 standards.

Validation answers: Are we modeling the right problem?  
Verification answers: Is the documentation logically sound, consistent, and complete?

==== *Validation Strategy*

Validation ensures that domain abstractions and derived requirements correspond to observed clinical scheduling behavior.

===== *Scenario-Based Stakeholder Validation*

**Scenario 1: Patient Requests Specialist Appointment**

**Actors:**  
- Patient: Ana Rivera, 35-year-old with new referral from primary care physician.  
- Provider: Dr. Luis Santiago, cardiologist at Central Health Clinic.  
- Staff: Administrative assistant Maria Torres managing appointment books.  

**Context:** Ana needs a cardiology appointment. Clinic rules require:  
1. Referral from primary care.  
2. Insurance pre-authorization for cardiology consultation.  
3. Available time slot within 14 days.  

**Walkthrough:**  

1. Ana contacts the clinic via phone; Maria checks `TimeSlotCatalog` for Dr. Santiago’s openings.  
2. Maria identifies a potential slot on April 12 at 10:30 AM.  
3. Ana provides referral; Maria verifies insurance coverage using `ClinicSystem`.  
4. Ana is offered the slot, but the insurance approval is pending.  
5. Ana asks to be placed on a waitlist for an earlier cancellation; Maria records this in `BehaviorTrace`.  
6. Two days later, another patient cancels. Ana is automatically notified and confirmed for April 10 at 11:00 AM.  

**Stakeholder Feedback:**  
- Ana emphasized that automatic waitlist assignment reduced frustration.  
- Maria noted that insurance verification often delays slot confirmation.  
- Dr. Santiago requested clarity on how pending approvals interact with waitlist logic.  

**Refinements Made:**  
- Introduced `PendingApproval` status for appointments awaiting insurance verification.  
- Clarified that `Waitlist` auto-confirmation applies only when `PendingApproval` is cleared.  
- Updated domain concept “Time Slot” to include occupancy status, waitlist queue, and referral dependencies.

===== *Terminology Validation*

All domain terms were reviewed for consistency:  

- **Patient**, **Provider** (instead of generic “Doctor”)  
- **Time Slot**, **Referral**, **Insurance Authorization**, **AppointmentBook**, **BehaviorTrace**, **Waitlist**  

**Identified Issues:**  

1. Requirements used "Doctor" instead of defined "Provider".  
2. `Specialty`, `ClinicSystem`, `AppointmentBook`, `TimeSlotCatalog` appeared in functions but were undefined in terminology.  
3. Conflicting logic:  
   - "Capacity handling per time slot" vs "single patient per slot" invariant.  
   - Waitlist auto-assignment vs pending approval → potential race condition.  

**Actions Taken:**  
- Standardized all mentions of "Doctor" → "Provider".  
- Added missing definitions to terminology for all system objects.  
- Introduced conditional rules: Waitlist assignment only after PendingApproval is cleared.  

===== *Verification Strategy*

Verification ensures requirements are consistent, complete, and traceable to domain concepts.

**Consistency Review:**  

- Checked that each requirement references defined terminology.  
- Identified and resolved contradictions:  
  1. Feature “Patients can automatically receive canceled slots” conflicted with “PendingApproval” → introduced explicit condition.  
  2. Feature “Appointments may enter PendingApproval for staff review” clarified in context of waitlist and auto-confirmation.  

**Traceability Verification:**  

For each requirement, verified:  

1. Originating domain concept.  
2. Stakeholder need it addresses.  
3. Structural tension it mitigates.  

*Example:*  

Domain Concept: Limited Provider Availability  
Requirement: “System must manage discrete Time Slots and enforce capacity constraints.”  
Traceability: Provider Availability → Time Slot → Capacity Constraint → Requirement.  

===== *Requirements Precision Check*

- Replaced vague terms (efficient, improved) with measurable criteria:  
  - “Prevent double-booking of Time Slots.”  
  - “Notify waitlist patients within 1 hour of slot availability.”  
- Ensured all requirements can be tested against system simulation or audit logs.

===== *Walkthrough-Based Verification*

**Peer Review & Inspection:**  

- Scenarios:  
  1. Cancellation after insurance approval.  
  2. Referral expiration before booking.  
  3. Simultaneous waitlist slot assignment attempts.  
  4. Overbooking edge cases.  

**Findings & Alternatives Considered:**  

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Issue | Alternatives Considered | Chosen Solution

| Missing terminology
a| Ignore, redefine, or standardize
a| Standardized “Doctor” → “Provider”; added missing definitions

| Conflicting slot assignment rules
a| Keep auto-confirmation, disable waitlist, conditional rules
a| Conditional waitlist assignment post-PendingApproval

| Ambiguous requirement language
a| Vague → measurable
a| Reformulated for verifiability

| Overlap in approval and waitlist
a| Locking, notifications, or status flags
a| Introduced `PendingApproval` status and notification workflow
|===

Walkthroughs confirmed logical gaps were resolved, with clearly defined alternative solutions and rationale.
