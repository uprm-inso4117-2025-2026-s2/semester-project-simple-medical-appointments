== *Appendix*

=== *AI Disclosure Statement*

==== Tools Used

Our team used Generative AI tools to help us with the development and documentation of the *Simple Medical Appointments* project. The tools we used are:

* *ChatGPT (GPT-5.2 Plus tier)*: We used this tool to help us structure our documentation make our requirements clearer and improve our user stories and personas. It also helped us come up with ideas for the architecture of the project.

* *GitHub Copilot*: This tool helped us with coding by generating code suggesting ways to improve our code and making implementation tasks faster.

These tools helped us be more productive organize our ideas and make our technical writing better. However they did not make our design decisions. Do the implementation work for us.

==== Stages of AI Usage

We used AI tools at stages of the project from the beginning to the end.

*Learning & Setup*

AI helped us fix configuration issues understand how the framework works and figure out implementation details when we were setting up the project.

*Brainstorming & Design*

We used AI to think of ways to design the system, database, scheduling logic and user interface. The ideas AI gave us were a starting point for our discussions, not the final decisions.

*Structuring & Refinement*

AI helped us organize our Epics, Features, User Stories and Personas according to the guidelines. It also helped us format. Clarify our AsciiDoc documentation.

*Documentation*

AI was used to:

- Make our wording and grammar better

- Make our requirements consistent

- Organize tables and traceability matrices

- Make our acceptance criteria clearer

Our team reviewed edited and approved all the documentation.

==== Human vs AI Contributions

AI tools gave us suggestions, templates and draft structures..

* Our team made all the important design decisions for the system.

* We. Verified all the functional requirements, scheduling logic and validation rules.

* We wrote, reviewed and tested all the implementation logic and integration decisions.

* We created all the diagrams, visual identity decisions and workflow definitions.

We always reviewed, modified and adapted the content generated by AI to fit the needs of the *Simple Medical Appointments* project.

There were tasks where we did not use AI all like debugging complex logical errors validating scheduling edge cases and resolving integration conflicts.

==== Verification and Conflict Resolution

We had a process to verify all the outputs that AI helped us with:

* We checked all the generated requirements against the milestone guidelines.

* We validated suggestions against official documentation and framework references.

* We tested code suggestions locally before integrating them.

* We had least one team member peer-review all the documentation drafts.

For example when AI-generated requirement structures did not align with our conventions we manually revised the identifiers and mappings to make them consistent.

==== Reflection

Using AI tools made us more efficient by speeding up drafting, formatting and early-stage idea generation. However we noticed that:

* Sometimes AI-generated AsciiDoc formatting needed to be corrected

* Some technical suggestions were too general. Needed to be adjusted for our specific domain.

* Human validation was necessary to ensure correctness and alignment with project goals.

We learned that AI is best used as a tool to help us be more productive not as a decision-maker. It supported our work. All the final design, analysis and implementation decisions were still made by our team.

AI tools, like ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot helped us. They did not replace critical thinking, understanding of the domain or collaborative decision-making. The *Simple Medical Appointments* project was a team effort and AI tools were one of the tools we used to make it happen.
