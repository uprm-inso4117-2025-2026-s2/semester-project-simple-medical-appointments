==== *Events, Actions, and Behaviors*

==== Domain Events

The following events are observable from the stakeholder perspective
and occur at specific moments in time.

*User has just been registered*  
A person has completed the process required to become a platform member.

*User has just been authenticated*  
A person’s credentials have been verified and access to the platform has been granted.

*Appointment slot has just become available*  
A provider or clinic has published or released a time period for scheduling.

*Appointment has just been requested*  
A patient has selected a slot and submitted a request for consultation.

*Appointment has just been confirmed*  
A scheduling request has been accepted and capacity has been reserved.

*Appointment has just been canceled*  
A previously scheduled consultation has been withdrawn by a patient or clinic.

*Appointment has just been rescheduled*  
A scheduled consultation has been moved to a different time.

*Patient has just been marked as no-show*  
A scheduled appointment time has passed without the patient attending.

*Provider availability has just been updated*  
A clinic or doctor has modified working hours or service capacity.

*Reminder has just been issued*  
A notification regarding an upcoming appointment has been sent.

*Slot has just been reassigned from waitlist*  
A previously occupied slot has been allocated to another waiting patient.

==== Actions

*Register user*  
A person provides information required to create an account.

*Authenticate user*  
A person submits credentials in order to access the system.

*Publish availability*  
A clinic or provider defines time blocks in which appointments can occur.

*Request appointment*  
A patient selects a time and submits a consultation request.

*Confirm appointment*  
The system or clinic validates a request and reserves capacity.

*Cancel appointment*  
A patient or clinic removes a scheduled consultation.

*Reschedule appointment*  
A patient or clinic changes the time of an existing appointment.

*Join waitlist*  
A patient expresses interest in earlier availability.

*Send reminder*  
The system dispatches a notification regarding a future appointment.

*Review schedule*  
A user inspects upcoming or past appointments.

==== Behaviors

*Managing access to care*  
A repeated pattern of users registering, authenticating, and interacting with
scheduling features to obtain medical consultations.

*Allocating limited appointment capacity*  
A continuous process of publishing availability, confirming requests,
and redistributing freed slots.

*Adjusting schedules dynamically*  
A recurring cycle of cancelations, rescheduling, and availability updates
driven by clinic workload and patient needs.

*Reducing missed appointments*  
A pattern of issuing reminders and tracking attendance outcomes
to improve schedule reliability over time.

*Balancing demand across providers*  
A repeated redistribution of patient requests through search,
waitlists, and reassignment mechanisms.
